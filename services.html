Services

The software that runs a business

Broad on purpose. Noctix takes on the operational software most businesses actually need — and builds it to a standard you can leave alone.

01-03 / The disciplines

01

Custom software

Web applications, dashboards, and internal tools shaped around how your business actually operates — not the other way around. Built lean, tested, and documented, with no platform lock-in and no framework bloat.

02

Automation

Operational pipelines that run without an operator: document processing, billing, reporting, scheduling. Verification gates guard anything that touches money, alerts fire only when a human is genuinely needed, and AI appears only where it earns its place.

03

Integrations

Your systems, finally talking to each other: accounting platforms, spreadsheets, inboxes, logistics and industry APIs tied into one coherent flow — so data moves without being retyped, and without quietly drifting out of sync.

04 / How we work

Built to hand over, not to hold hostage.

A system you can't run without the person who built it isn't finished. Noctix optimises for the day you stop needing us in the room.

05 / What you can expect

Three commitments.

Production-grade by default

Automated tests and CI on everything we ship. The flagship platform runs 105 tests before any release reaches the machine that touches money.

Plain language

You'll always know what the system does, what it costs, and why it's built that way. English or Spanish, whichever suits your team.

Yours when it's done

Documentation, controls, and alerting designed so the system keeps running without us — and so someone else could pick it up if they had to.

Not sure which of these you need?

Most projects are a mix. Describe the problem in your own words and we'll propose the smallest system that actually solves it.
