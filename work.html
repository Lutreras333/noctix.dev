The problem

Billing lived across email, spreadsheets, and QuickBooks. Every flight meant reading shipment manifests and vendor invoices out of inboxes, transcribing them into ledgers with live formulas, and keying transactions in one at a time — hours of daily work, with real money riding on every keystroke.

What Noctix built

A production system that runs the entire billing cycle on its own. It detects flights through the logistics platform's API, ingests shipment manifests and vendor PDFs, writes formatted ledgers with live formulas, and generates customer invoices and vendor bills in QuickBooks — end to end, with no operator at the keyboard.

Because the system moves real money, verification comes first: every write is re-checked against source documents before it executes. If the numbers don't reconcile, the transaction halts and a human is alerted. A deterministic core does the work; an AI exception-handler is summoned only when something looks wrong.

  • Autonomous cycles twice a day on a self-administered VPS, with tiered email alerts (ok / check / problems)
  • Verification gates that re-check every write against source documents
  • Billing weights the vendor API doesn't expose, reconstructed exact to the kilogram — 470 of 470 shipments
  • Six ledgers across two country pipelines, written and reconciled automatically
  • Machine-gated financial controls in place of manual approval
  • One-script migration to a new machine
