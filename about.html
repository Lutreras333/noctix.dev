About

Named for the night shift.

Noctix is the software studio of Lucas Utreras Acevedo, a software engineer who builds production systems that run business operations autonomously — most recently a billing platform trusted with six figures a month, invoicing on its own twice a day.

The name comes from nox — Latin for night. The real test of operational software is what happens while nobody's watching: it should keep working, verify its own output, and only wake a human when something genuinely needs one. That's the standard every Noctix system is built to.

Lucas works in English and Spanish, with clients and their teams in both.
